How Auto-Sync Works
1. Offline Operation

When your internet goes down, the Windows App continues billing with its local SQLite engine. Bills are marked with PENDING status.

2. Connection Detection

The Windows background worker polls /api/health/ every few seconds. Once internet connectivity is restored, it detects the server immediately.

3. Auto-Push & Reconcile

Pending bills are atomically batched and posted to /api/sync/push/. Stock is automatically decremented on the MySQL cloud server.
